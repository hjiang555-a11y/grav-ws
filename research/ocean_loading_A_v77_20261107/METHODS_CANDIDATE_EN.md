### Separability of the solid-Earth and ocean-loading responses

We assessed whether the observations distinguish the two physical contributions within the lunar semidiurnal group. We replaced its combined template by separate solid-Earth and ocean-loading templates, retaining the neighbouring semidiurnal and auxiliary diurnal groups, the fixed remainder, the same 54 segment constants and the original weights. After projecting out the other groups and constants, the two source templates have a weighted correlation of 0.941. Their normalized condition number is 5.74; the loading component orthogonal to the solid template retains 11.4% of its squared norm and has an rms amplitude of 0.298×10⁻¹⁸ on this sampling. The separate response scales are 1.21±0.81 for the solid contribution and 0.47±2.01 for loading, using the larger of scaled least-squares and UTC-day jackknife standard errors. A restricted residual sign-flip diagnostic over eight periods gives a two-sided probability of 0.844 for zero lunar loading. The joint source fit therefore does not resolve loading independently. Fixing the solid scale to unity gives a conditional loading scale of 0.96±0.44, which answers a different, theory-constrained question. These source diagnostics use the same observations as the primary semidiurnal measurement.
