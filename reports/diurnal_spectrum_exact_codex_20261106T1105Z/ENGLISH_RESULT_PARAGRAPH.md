The same measured-record scan contains structure in the diurnal band, including a local maximum near K₁. Its interpretation differs from the semidiurnal display. At the nominal K₁ frequency, subtracting the fixed full tidal prediction reduces scan power by 69%, but subtracting only its semidiurnal component already reduces that power by 64%. The observation window therefore couples the strong semidiurnal prediction into this frequency region. At P₁, full-template subtraction instead approximately doubles the scan power. P₁ and K₁ each lie about one inverse-record-length interval from exact 24 h, and the P₁/24-h peak association changes under some baseline and weighting choices. These diagnostics identify diurnal-band structure but do not provide separately calibrated detections of individual diurnal constituents. The residual spectrum contains measurement noise together with possible model error and unmodelled signals.
