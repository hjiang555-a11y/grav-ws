**Observed and tide-subtracted spectra in equivalent potential units.** **a**, Generalized sinusoidal spectra of the 3,299 accepted ten-minute observations over a 353.39-day span, before and after subtraction of the fixed full tidal prediction. **b**, The same spectra in the semidiurnal band; dashed lines mark the nominal M₂ and S₂ frequencies. The prediction is subtracted at the observed timestamps with unit response, without re-estimating tidal amplitudes or phases. Missing intervals remain empty and the calendar is not compressed. Both spectra use equal weights and 54 segment constants, with identical nuisance projection for observations and trial sinusoids. Equivalent potential density is obtained from the fractional-frequency density by multiplying by c⁴, where c = 299,792,458 m s⁻¹. The ordinate retains the nominal-600-s one-sided Lomb–Scargle density convention; it is not an integral-calibrated continuous-year variance. The residual describes measurement noise relative to the specified tide model and may contain model error or unmodelled signals. These are the same observations used for the tidal analysis, not an independent experiment or a new detection-probability calibration. Sampling aliases are shown rather than removed from the display.
