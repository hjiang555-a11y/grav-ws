Dear Editors,

We present *Tidal potential measured on a coastal–inland optical-clock baseline* for consideration as a Nature Article. A 1,350-km fibre link connects clocks in Shanghai and Wuhan. Across eight observing periods spanning a year, their changing lunar–solar phase coverage allows two neighbouring semidiurnal responses to be estimated jointly: $0.99\pm0.12$ for M₂+N₂ and $1.13\pm0.29$ for S₂+K₂. Ocean loading contributes approximately one third of the predicted variation. The measurement tests the combined potential; it does not claim an independent loading measurement.

The summer observations overlap the absolute Yb/Sr frequency-ratio study by Hou et al. (arXiv:2610.34250). This Article addresses tidal-component separation in the extended interrupted record, without re-reporting that ratio or its earlier tidal-correlation significance.

We cite Stahl et al. (arXiv:2610.39635) as independent, contemporaneous and complementary observations on European baselines.
