**Equivalent-potential and fractional-frequency spectra of the clock comparison.** a, Full displayed band; b, semidiurnal region. The same 3,299 measured bins are used in both curves, retaining their original timestamps, equal weights and 54 floating segment means; gaps are not filled. Blue shows the observed record. Orange shows the time-domain residual after subtraction of the fixed unit-amplitude tidal prediction, without re-estimating its amplitude or phase. Both density axes are expressed per microhertz. The left axis gives equivalent-potential density in (m²/s²)²/µHz; the right axis gives fractional-frequency density in 10⁻³⁵/µHz, with the exact relation S_W = c⁴ S_y. The axes describe the same curves. The nominal one-sided least-squares normalization uses 600 s. Segment projection and the sampling window prevent direct interpretation of the scan integral as continuous-year variance. Dashed lines mark the astronomical M₂ and S₂ frequencies. The residual shares the observations and can contain model error and unmodelled signals; peak suppression is not an independent detection test.
